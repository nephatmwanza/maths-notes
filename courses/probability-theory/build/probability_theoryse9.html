9 Martingales

The course outline promises martingale theory, and it belongs here: a martingale is a stochastic process defined by a conditional expectation, so it draws on the conditioning of Chapter 4 and the convergence modes of Chapter 6 at once. The idea is the mathematical form of a fair game.
