12.3 Review: which method for which question

Table 2 collects the methods of the course by the question they answer. Three habits run through all of them: account for censoring rather than discarding it; state what the time origin and the event are; and check the assumptions of a model, proportional hazards above all, before reporting its single summary numbers.
