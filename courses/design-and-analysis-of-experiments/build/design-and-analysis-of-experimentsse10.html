10 Two-level fractional factorial designs

As the number of factors grows, a full \(2^k\) design quickly becomes impractical, and most of its runs are spent estimating high-order interactions that the sparsity of effects principle says are negligible. A \(2^6\) design has \(64\) runs, but only \(6\) of its \(63\) degrees of freedom are for main effects and \(15\) for two-factor interactions. A fractional factorial design runs only a carefully chosen fraction of the combinations, and gives up information on the high-order interactions in exchange. These designs are the workhorse of screening experiments.
