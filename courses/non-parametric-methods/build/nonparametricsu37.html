4.3 Two Sample Test

The two sample K-S test is used to test whether the 2 samples come from the same distribution.
The procedure is similar to the one-sample case.
Suppose that the first sample has size \(m\) with an assumed cdf \(F(x)\) and the second sample has size \(n\) with cdf \(G(x)\). Define the statistic \[D_{m,n}=\sqrt {\frac {mn}{m+n}}\,\,\sup _{x\in \mathbb {R}}|F_m(x)-G_n(x)|\] Then we reject the hypothesis \(H_0:F(x)=G(x)\) for all \(x\). vs \(H_a:F(x)\neq G(x)\) for some \(x\).

If \(D_{m,n}>D_{m,n}(\alpha )\) where \(D_{m,n}(\alpha )\) is the critical value.
