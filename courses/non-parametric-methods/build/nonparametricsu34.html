3.25 Tests

1.
For \(H_0:\rho _s\leq 0\hspace {0.3cm}\text {vs}\hspace {0.3cm}H_a:\rho _s>0\) reject \(H_0\) if \(r_s>0\) and greater than the C.V for \(\alpha /2\).
2.
For \(H_0:\rho _s\geq 0\hspace {0.3cm}\text {vs}\hspace {0.3cm}H_a:\rho _s<0\); reject \(H_0\) if \(r_s<0\) and if its absolute value \(|r_s|\) is greater than C.V for \(\alpha /2\).
3.
For \(20<n<40\), compute \(\displaystyle {t=\frac {r_s}{S}\hspace {0.5cm}\text {where}\hspace {0.5cm} S=\Bigg (\frac {1-r^2_s}{n-s}\Bigg )}\hspace {0.2cm}\) for which two and one-tailed t(statistic t- distribution), for \(df=n-2\) are formulated.

Equivalently, you may employ \[F=\frac {1+|r_s|}{1-|r_s|}\thicksim F_{(n-2,n-2)}\] referring to two and one tailed C.V of F-distribution for numerator and denominator are degrees of freedom \(df=n-2\), C.V \(=F_{(\alpha ,n-2,n-2)}\).

4.
For \(n\geq 40\) i.e larger samples use \(Z=r_s(n-1)^{\frac {1}{2}},\hspace {1cm}Z\thicksim N(0,1)\)

\[\gamma _s=1-\frac {6\sum d_i^2}{n(n^2-n)}\]

Test

-
\(H_0:\rho \geq 0\) vs \(H_a:\rho _s<0\).
-
Rejection region: \(H_0\) is rejected if \(\gamma _s<0\) and \(|\gamma _s|\) is greater than the C.V for \(\alpha /2\).
-
\begin {align*} \sum &=8\\\\ n^3-n &=125-5=120\\\\ \gamma _s &=1-\frac {6\times 8}{120}\\ &=1-\frac {48}{120}\\ &=0.6\\ \end {align*}
\(\rightarrow \)
C.V \(\gamma _{s(\alpha ,n)}=\gamma _{s(0.025,5)}=1\)
\(\rightarrow \)
\(\gamma _s=0.6\); \(\gamma _{s(0.05,5)}=1.00\)
\(\rightarrow \)
fail to reject \(H_0\).
