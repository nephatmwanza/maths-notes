3.1 Wilcoxon Signed Rank Test

- Applicable to paired differences.

Example:

\[T_+=|2|+|6|+|2|+|2|=12\] \[T_-=|5|+|4|=9\] \[T_+=12\hspace {1cm}\text {and}\hspace {1cm} T_-=9\] \(H_0\): \(D_1\) and \(D_2\) are identical.

1.
\(H_a\): \(D_1\) is shifted to the left of \(D_2\).
2.
\(H_a\): \(D_1\) shifted to the right of \(D_2\).
3.
\(H_a\): \(D_1\) is shifted to the right or left of \(D_2\).

Test statistics:

1.
Test statistic = \(T_-=9\).
2.
Test statistic = \(T_+=12\).
3.
Test statistic = \(T=\min (T_+,T_-)=9\).

Rejection Region

i
\(T_+\leq T_0\)
ii
\(T_-\leq T_0\)
iii
\(T\leq T_0\)

Test \(H_0\) at \(\alpha =0.05\)

C.V \(\hspace {0.4cm} \implies \hspace {0.5cm} W_{(0.05,6)}=3=T_0\) \[W_{(0.05,6)}T_0=3/\hspace {0.3cm} T_-=9\] \[W_{(0.05,6)}T_0=3/\hspace {0.3cm} T_+=12\] \[W_{(0.05/2,6)}T_0=1/\hspace {0.3cm} T=9\] Decision: failed to reject \(H_0\).

Large Sample Approximation
For \(n\geq 25\), \(T_+\) is often standardised and normal approximation is used to obtain \(P\)-values and C.I for standardised test statistics e.g when \(H_0\) is true the expected value and the variance of \(T_+\) can be shown that \[E(T_+)=\frac {n(n+1)}{4};\hspace {1cm} \text {var}(T_+)=\frac {n(n+1)(2n+1)}{24}\] It can be shown that the random quantity is \[T^{*}=\frac {T_+-E(T_+)}{\sqrt {\text {var}(T_+)}}=\frac {T_+-\dfrac {n(n+1)}{4}}{\sqrt {\dfrac {n(n+1)(2n+1)}{24}}}\thicksim N(0,1)\]
