6.3 Even and Odd functions

Definition 6.16.

A function \(f\) is called an even function if \(f(-x)=f(x)\).

e.g the function \(f(x)=x^2\) is even since \(f(-x)=(-x)^2=x^2=f(x)\).

Definition 6.17.

A function \(f\) is called an odd function if \(f(-x)=-f(x)\).

e.g \(f(x)=x^3+x\) is odd. Since \begin {align*} f(-x) & = (-x)^3+(-x)=-x^3-x\\ & = -(x^3+x)\\ & = -f(x)\\ \end {align*}

Example 6.18.

Determine whether each of the following functions is even, odd or neither

1.
\(f(x)=3x^4-2x^2\)
2.
\(f(x)=x^3+x-2\)
3.
\(f(x)=2x^3-\dfrac {1}{x}\)

Solution.

1.
\(f(x)=3x^4-2x^2\) \begin {align*} \implies \hspace {0.5cm} f(-x) & = 3(-x)^4-2(-x)^2\\ & = 3x^4-2x^2\\ & = f(x) \end {align*}

i.e \(f(-x)=f(x)\) which implies that \(f(x)=3x^4-2x^2\) is even.

2.
\(f(x)=x^3+x-2\) \begin {align*} \implies \hspace {0.5cm} f(-x) & = (-x)^3+(-x)-2\\ & = -x^3-x-2\\ & = -(x^3+x+2) \end {align*}

Not even and not odd.

3.
\(f(x)=2x^3-\dfrac {1}{x}\) \begin {align*} \implies \hspace {0.5cm} f(-x) & = 2(-x)^3-\frac {1}{(-x)}\\ & =-2x^3+\frac {1}{x}\\ & = -\big (2x^3-\frac {1}{x}\big )\\ & = -f(x)\hspace {0.5cm}\text {it is odd}\\ \end {align*}
