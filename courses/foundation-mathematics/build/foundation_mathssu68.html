26.4 Scalar Multiplication of a Vector

A vector can be multiplied by a scalar (i.e a constant) such as \(\hspace {0.2cm} 2\underline {a}, \hspace {0.3cm} 3\underline {b}, \hspace {0.3cm} -3\underline {a}, \hspace {0.3cm} 5\underline {b}\)

In general, the vector \(\lambda \underline {a}\) where \(\lambda > 0\) is in the same direction as \(\underline {a}\), but its magnitude is \(\lambda \) time \(\begin {vmatrix} \underline {a}\\ \end {vmatrix}\).
