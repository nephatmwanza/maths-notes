22.6 Contact of Circles

1.
Two circles touch externally if the distance between their center is equal to the sum of their radii \(\hspace {0.2cm}(d = c_1c_2)\hspace {0.2cm}\), \(\hspace {0.2cm} d = r_1 + r_2\)
2.
The circles touch (or are tangency) internally if \(\hspace {0.2cm} d = r_1 - r_2\).
3.
The circles do not intersect if \(\hspace {0.2cm} d> r_1 + r_2\hspace {0.2cm}\) (each lies outside the other) or if \(\hspace {0.2cm} d< \left |r_1 - r_2\right |\) (one lies wholly inside the other).
4.
The circles intersect at two distinct points if \(\hspace {0.2cm}\left |r_1 - r_2\right | < d < r_1 + r_2\)
5.
Two circles are said to be orthogonal if they cut at right angles

The two circles are orthogonal if \(\hspace {0.2cm}d^2 = r_1^2 + r_2^2\)
