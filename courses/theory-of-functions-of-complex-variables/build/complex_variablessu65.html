11.4 Contour Integration and Cauchy’s Theorem

Problem 11.25. Let \(\gamma \) be a rectifiable curve and \(\varphi \) a continuous function on \(\gamma \). Define \[f(z) = \int _{\gamma }\dfrac {\varphi (w)}{w - z}\,dw \hspace {0.6cm}\text {for } z \in \mathbb {C}\setminus \{\gamma \}.\] Show that \(f(z)\) is continuous.

Problem 11.26. Evaluate \[\int _{\left |z\right | = 1} \dfrac {\left |dz\right |}{\left |z - \tfrac {1}{2}\right |^{2}}\] and show your work.

Problem 11.27. Let \(\gamma \) be a rectifiable curve and \(\phi \) a function continuous on \(\{\gamma \}\). Define, for \(z \in \mathbb {C}\setminus \{\gamma \}\), \[f(z) = \int _{\gamma }\dfrac {\phi (w)}{w - z}\,dw .\] Prove that \(f\) is continuous on \(\mathbb {C}\setminus \{\gamma \}\).

Problem 11.28. Evaluate \[\oint _{C}\dfrac {e^{\pi z}}{z(z + 2i)}\,dz,\] where \(C\) is the circle of radius \(3\) centred at the origin, oriented counter-clockwise.

Problem 11.29. Let \(C\) be the circle \(\left |z\right | = 3\), oriented counter-clockwise. Show that if \[g(z) = \int _{C}\dfrac {2s^{2} - s - 2}{s - z}\,ds \hspace {0.5cm}\left (\left |z\right | \neq 3\right ),\] then \(g(2) = 8\pi i\). What is the value of \(g(z)\) when \(\left |z\right | > 3\)?

Problem 11.30. Evaluate the integral \[\int ^{\infty }_{0}\dfrac {1}{x^{4} + 1}\,dx .\]

Problem 11.31. Evaluate \(\displaystyle \int _{\gamma }\dfrac {\log z}{z^{2} - 25}\,dz\), where \(\gamma \) parametrises \(\partial B(4,2)\) once counter-clockwise and \(\log z\) is the principal branch of the logarithm.

Problem 11.32. Let \(\gamma \subseteq \mathbb {C}\) be a piecewise differentiable curve, and let \(\overline {\gamma }\) be the image of \(\gamma \) under the map \(z \mapsto \overline {z}\).

(a).
If \(f\) is continuous on \(\overline {\gamma }\), show that \(z \mapsto \overline {f(\overline {z})}\) is continuous and that \[\int _{\overline {\gamma }} f(z)\,dz = \overline {\int _{\gamma }\overline {f(\overline {z})}\,dz} .\]
(b).
As an application of (a), show that if \(\gamma \) is the positively oriented unit circle then \[\int _{\left |z\right | = 1} f(z)\,dz = -\int _{\left |z\right | = 1}\overline {f\!\left (\overline {z}\right )}\, \dfrac {dz}{z^{2}} .\]

Problem 11.33. Using contour integration, find the value of \[\int ^{2\pi }_{0}\dfrac {dt}{2 - \cos t} .\]

Problem 11.34. State and prove Morera’s Theorem.

Problem 11.35. Let \(p(z)\) be a polynomial. Show that \[\int _{C} p(z)\,d\overline {z} = -2\pi i R^{2}p'(0),\] where \(C\) denotes the circle \(\left |z\right | = R\) winding counter-clockwise once.

Problem 11.36. Evaluate the integral \[\int ^{\infty }_{0}\dfrac {x \sin x}{x^{2} + a^{2}}\,dx,\] where \(a\) is a real number.

Problem 11.37.

(a).
State Cauchy’s Theorem on a simply connected region.
(b).
Suppose \(G\) is simply connected and \(f\) is analytic on \(G\). Show that there is an analytic function \(F\) on \(G\) with \(\dfrac {dF}{dz} = f\).

Problem 11.38. Assume \(f(z)\) is holomorphic for \(\left |z\right | < R\), where \(R > 1\). Prove that \[\int ^{2\pi }_{0} f\!\left (e^{it}\right )\cos ^{2}\dfrac {t}{2}\,dt = \pi f(0) + \dfrac {\pi }{2}f'(0),\] \[\int ^{2\pi }_{0} f\!\left (e^{it}\right )\sin ^{2}\dfrac {t}{2}\,dt = \pi f(0) - \dfrac {\pi }{2}f'(0).\]

Problem 11.39. Prove that \(\displaystyle \int ^{\infty }_{0}\dfrac {\sin ^{2}x}{x^{2}}\,dx = \dfrac {\pi }{2}\).

Problem 11.40. Let \(a > 0\) be a fixed real number. Compute the line integral \[\int _{\gamma }\dfrac {ds}{x^{2} + y^{2} - 2ax + a^{2}},\] where \(\gamma \) is the circle of radius \(\rho > a\) about the origin, oriented counter-clockwise, and \(s\) is arc-length.

Problem 11.41. Suppose \(f\) is analytic inside and on a positively oriented simple closed contour \(\gamma \), and \(f\) has no zero on \(\gamma \). Show that if \(f\) has \(n\) zeros \(\{z_k\}^{n}_{k=1}\) inside \(\gamma \), the multiplicity of \(z_k\) being \(m_k\), then \[\int _{\gamma }\dfrac {z f'(z)}{f(z)}\,dz = 2\pi i\left (\sum ^{n}_{k = 1} m_k z_k\right ).\]

Problem 11.42. Show that \(\displaystyle \int ^{\infty }_{0}\dfrac {\cos x}{1 + x^{2}}\,dx = \dfrac {1}{2}\cdot \dfrac {\pi }{e}\).

Problem 11.43. Evaluate the integral \[\int _{\gamma }\dfrac {z^{1/m}}{(z - 1)^{m}}\,dz,\] where \(\gamma (t) = 1 + \tfrac {1}{2}e^{it}\) and \(m\) is a positive integer.

Problem 11.44.

(a).
State Cauchy’s Theorem on a simply connected region.
(b).
Suppose \(G\) is simply connected and \(f\) is analytic on \(G\). Show that there is an analytic function \(F\) on \(G\) with \(\dfrac {dF}{dz} = f\).

Problem 11.45. Calculate \(\displaystyle \int _{\gamma } z^{-1/2}\,dz\), where \(\gamma \) is the straight line from \(+1\) to \(-i\), then from \(-i\) to \(-1\).

Problem 11.46. Evaluate the integral \[\int ^{\infty }_{0}\dfrac {dx}{(x^{2} + 1)^{2}} .\]
