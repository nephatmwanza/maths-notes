7.6 Schwarz Lemma

i.
If \(\hspace {0.2cm} f(z) = 0,\hspace {0.2cm} f(z)\hspace {0.1cm}\) is analytic for \(\hspace {0.1cm}\begin {vmatrix} z\\ \end {vmatrix} < 1\hspace {0.1cm}\) and \(\hspace {0.1cm} \begin {vmatrix} f(z)\\ \end {vmatrix}< 1,\hspace {0.1cm}\) then \(\hspace {0.1cm} \begin {vmatrix} f(z)\\ \end {vmatrix}\leq \begin {vmatrix} z\\ \end {vmatrix}\hspace {0.1cm}\) and \(\hspace {0.1cm} \begin {vmatrix} f'(0)\\ \end {vmatrix}\leq 1\)

Moreover

ii.
If \(\hspace {0.1cm} \begin {vmatrix} f(z)\\ \end {vmatrix} = \begin {vmatrix} z\\ \end {vmatrix}\hspace {0.1cm}\) at an interior of \(\hspace {0.1cm} \begin {vmatrix} z\\ \end {vmatrix} = 1\), then \(\hspace {0.1cm} f(z) = ze^{i\alpha }\hspace {0.1cm}\) with \(\alpha \) real.
