11.11 The Riemann Mapping Theorem and Conformal Equivalence

This set goes beyond the material of these notes.

Problem 11.133. Let \(G\) be a simply connected region in \(\mathbb {C}\) with \(G \neq \mathbb {C}\). Without using the Riemann mapping theorem, prove that there exists a non-constant bounded analytic function \(f : G \rightarrow \mathbb {C}\).

Problem 11.134. Consider each of the following three domains:

(a).
the annulus \(\{z \in \mathbb {C} : 1 < \left |z\right | < 2\}\);
(b).
the complex plane \(\mathbb {C}\);
(c).
the region \(S = \mathbb {C}\setminus \{z \in \mathbb {C} : \operatorname {Im}(z) = 0,\ \operatorname {Re}(z) > 1\}\).

Determine in each case whether it is possible to find a one-to-one analytic function mapping the given domain onto the unit disc \(B(0,1) = \{z : \left |z\right | < 1\}\). If it is not possible, give reasons; if it is, produce the function.

Problem 11.135. Let \(G\) be a simply connected region which is not the whole plane, and suppose \(\overline {z} \in G\) whenever \(z \in G\). Let \(a \in G \cap \mathbb {R}\) and suppose \(f : G \rightarrow D\) is a one-to-one analytic function with \(f(a) = 0\), \(f'(a) > 0\) and \(f(G) = D\). Let \(G^{+} = \{z \in G : \operatorname {Im}(z) > 0\}\). Show that \(f(G^{+})\) must lie entirely above or entirely below the real axis.

Problem 11.136. Let \(G\) be a simply connected region which is not the whole plane. Without applying the Riemann Mapping Theorem, prove directly that there exists a non-constant analytic map \(f : G \rightarrow \mathbb {C}\) such that \(\mathbb {C}\setminus f(G)\) contains a non-empty open set.

Problem 11.137.

(a).
State the Maximum Modulus Principle for an analytic function.
(b).
State the classical Schwarz Lemma for an analytic function \(f : \Delta \rightarrow \Delta \), where \(\Delta \) is the unit disc and \(f(0) = 0\). Give a proof using part (a).
(c).
Prove the uniqueness part of the Riemann Mapping Theorem.

Problem 11.138. Let \(G\) be a simply connected region which is not the whole plane. Without applying the Riemann mapping theorem, prove directly that there exists a non-constant analytic map \(f : G \rightarrow D\), the unit disc.

Problem 11.139. Find explicitly a holomorphic bijection between \(G = \{z : \left |z\right | < 1 \text { and } \operatorname {Re}(z) > 0\}\) and the open unit disc \(D = \{z : \left |z\right | < 1\}\).

Problem 11.140. Determine whether there exists a one-to-one, onto analytic mapping between the pair of domains \(A\) and \(B\) of \(\mathbb {C}\) in each case. Give reasons.

(a).
\(A = \mathbb {C}\), the whole plane; \(B = \{z \in \mathbb {C} : \left |z\right | < 100\}\).
(b).
\(A = \{z \in \mathbb {C} : \left |z\right | < 1\}\); \(B = \{x + iy : x, y \in \mathbb {R},\ \left |x\right | < 1,\ \left |y\right | < 2\}\).
(c).
\(A = \mathbb {C}\setminus \{0\}\); \(B = \mathbb {C}\setminus \{1, 2\}\).
(d).
\(A = \{z \in \mathbb {C} : \left |z\right | < 1\}\); \(B = \mathbb {C}\setminus \{x + iy : y \in \mathbb {R},\ x = 0,\ y \geq 0\}\).

Problem 11.141.

(a).
Show that every conformal one-to-one map \(w(z)\) of the unit disc \(\left |z\right | < 1\) onto itself is a linear fractional transformation. You may assume that \(w(z)\) extends continuously to the boundary \(\left |z\right | = 1\), on which \(\left |w\right | = 1\).
(b).
Show that there is no conformal map of \(\mathbb {C}\) onto \(\left |z\right | < 1\).

Problem 11.142.

(a).
State the Riemann Mapping Theorem.
(b).
Explain why its conclusion does not apply to the complex plane \(\mathbb {C}\) or to the punctured disc \(B(0;1)\setminus \{0\}\).

Problem 11.143.

(a).
State the Riemann Mapping Theorem.
(b).
Prove the uniqueness part of the Riemann Mapping Theorem.
(c).
Describe the Riemann mapping \(f : H \rightarrow \Delta \), where \(H = \{x + iy : x > 0\}\) and \(\Delta = \{z \in \mathbb {C} : \left |z\right | < 1\}\), such that \(f(1) = 0\).
