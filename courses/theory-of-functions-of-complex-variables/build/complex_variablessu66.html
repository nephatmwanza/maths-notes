11.5 Liouville’s Theorem and Entire Functions

Problem 11.47. Determine all entire functions \(f(z)\) with the property \(\left |f(z)\right | \leq \left |e^{z}\right |\).

Problem 11.48. Determine all entire functions \(f(z)\) with the property that \(\left |f(z)\right | \leq \left |e^{z}\right |\) whenever \(\left |z\right | > 1\).

Problem 11.49. Let \(f\) be entire with \(\left |f(z)\right | < M\left |z\right |^{5}\) for all \(\left |z\right | > R\), where \(M\) and \(R\) are positive constants. Prove that \(f\) is a polynomial of degree not greater than \(5\).

Problem 11.50. State and prove Liouville’s Theorem.

Problem 11.51. If \(f\) is entire and \(z^{-1}\operatorname {Re}(f(z)) \rightarrow 0\) as \(z \rightarrow \infty \), show that \(f\) is constant.

Problem 11.52. Let \(f\) be entire, let \(n \geq 0\) be an integer, and let \(M, R > 0\). Show that if \(\left |f(z)\right | \leq M\left |z\right |^{n}\) for all \(\left |z\right | \geq R\), then \(f\) is a polynomial of degree at most \(n\).

Problem 11.53. Let \(f\) be entire with \(\left |f(z)\right | < M\left |z\right |^{\alpha }\) for all \(z \in \mathbb {C}\) with \(\left |z\right | > R\), where \(M\), \(R\) and \(\alpha \) are constants with \(0 < \alpha < 1\). Prove that \(f\) is constant.

Problem 11.54. Let \(f\) be entire and suppose \(f(z) = f\!\left (\dfrac {1}{z}\right )\) for all \(z \neq 0\). Prove that \(f\) is constant.

Problem 11.55. Let \(f(z)\) be entire. Assume there is a natural number \(n\) and real numbers \(R, M > 0\) such that \[\left |f(z)\right | \leq M\left |z\right |^{n} \hspace {0.5cm}\text {for all } \left |z\right | \geq R.\] Prove that \(f(z)\) is a polynomial of degree \(n\) or less.

Problem 11.56. If \(f(z)\) is entire and \(z^{-1}\operatorname {Re}(f(z)) \rightarrow 0\) as \(z \rightarrow \infty \), show that \(f(z)\) is constant.

Problem 11.57. Let \(n \geq 1\) be an integer and let \(f(z)\) be entire with \(\left |f(z)\right | \leq \left |z\right |^{n}\) for all \(z \in \mathbb {C}\). Prove that there is a constant \(c\) with \(f(z) = cz^{n}\).

Problem 11.58. \(f(z)\) is entire and \(\left |f(z)\right | \leq e^{x}\) throughout the plane, where \(x = \operatorname {Re}(z)\). What can be said about \(f(z)\)?

Problem 11.59. If \(w(z)\) is entire and \(p(z)\) is a polynomial with \(\left |w(z)\right | \leq \left |p(z)\right |\) for all \(z \in \mathbb {C}\), show that \(w(z)\) is a constant multiple of \(p(z)\).

Problem 11.60.

(a).
State Liouville’s Theorem.
(b).
\(f(z)\) is entire and \(\left |f(z)\right | \leq e^{x}\) throughout the plane, where \(x = \operatorname {Re}(z)\). What can be said about \(f(z)\)?
