3.1 Generating Open Sets

1.
The empty set is open
2.
The entire complex plane is an open set.
3.
If \(\, S_1, \, S_2\,\) are open sets then \(S_1 \cap S_2\) is open.
4.
If \(\big \{S_{\alpha }\big \}_{\alpha \in A}\,\) (\(A\) is some index set) is an arbitrary collection open sets in \(\mathbb {C}\) then \(\, \bigcup \limits _{\alpha \in A}S_{\alpha }\,\) is an open set.

Example

1.
\(\, \big \{z \in \mathbb {C}:\, \left |z - a\right | > r\big \}\,\) is an open set.
2.
\(\, \big \{z \in \mathbb {C}:\, \left |z - a\right | > r_1\big \} \cap \big \{z \in \mathbb {C}:\, \left |z - a\right | < r_2\big \}\,\) where \(\, r_1 < r_2\).

\(= \big \{z \in \mathbb {C}:\,r_1 < \left |z - a\right | < r_2\big \}\,\) is an open set.

Exercise 3.6. \(\pi = \big \{ z \in \mathbb {C}:\, \operatorname {Im}(z) > 0\big \}\,\) is open.
