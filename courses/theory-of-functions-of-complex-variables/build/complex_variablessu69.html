The reading list set with the syllabus

1).
L. Ahlfors, Complex Analysis, McGraw–Hill.
2).
J. Conway, Functions of One Complex Variable, 2nd edition, Springer.
3).
K. Knopp, Theory of Functions, Parts I and II; Problem Book, Volumes I and II, Dover.
4).
R. Narasimhan, Complex Analysis in One Variable, Birkhäuser.
5).
W. Rudin, Principles of Mathematical Analysis, 3rd edition, McGraw–Hill (undergraduate material, chapters 6 and 7 only).
6).
S. Saks and A. Zygmund, Analytic Functions, Warsaw.

Conway is the closest of these to the present notes and is already in the bibliography; Ahlfors is the standard reference for the material in sections 7 and 8 above.
