11.8 Singularities, Laurent Series and Residues

Problem 11.88. Give the Laurent series expansion of \[f(z) = \dfrac {1}{1 - z^{2}}\] on

(a).
the annulus \(0 < \left |z\right | < 1\);
(b).
the annulus \(1 < \left |z\right | < 2\).

Problem 11.89. Find the Laurent series of \(f(z) = \dfrac {1}{z(z-1)(z-2)}\) about the origin in the annulus \(\{z \in \mathbb {C} : 1 < \left |z\right | < 2\}\).

Problem 11.90. Let \(G\) be a region and \(a \in G\). Suppose \(f\) is continuous on \(G\) and analytic on \(G\setminus \{a\}\). Prove that \(f\) is analytic at \(a\).

Problem 11.91. Give Laurent series expansions of \[f(z) = \dfrac {1}{z^{2}(1 - z)}\] in powers of \(z\) on two non-empty, non-intersecting annuli, and specify the maximal region on which each expansion is valid.

Problem 11.92. Let \(G\) be a region and \(a \in G\). Suppose \(f\) is continuous on \(G\) and analytic on \(G\setminus \{a\}\). Prove that \(f\) is analytic at \(a\).

Problem 11.93. Suppose \(\varphi (z)\) is analytic in a domain \(D\) and let \(a \in D\). Prove that there exists a unique function \(f(z)\) analytic on \(D\) such that, on \(D\setminus \{a\}\), \[f(z) = \dfrac {\varphi (z) - \varphi (a)}{z - a}.\]

Problem 11.94. Let \(f = u + iv\) be analytic on \(0 < \left |z\right | < 210\) with \(2011 < u < 2012\). Prove that \(f\) can be analytically extended to \(\{z \in \mathbb {C} : \left |z\right | < 210\}\).

Problem 11.95. Find the poles and residues of \(\dfrac {\cos z}{\sin z}\).

Problem 11.96. Find the Laurent series of \[f(z) = \dfrac {1}{z(z-1)(z-3)}\] about the origin in \(\{z : 1 < \left |z\right | < 3\}\).

Problem 11.97. Let \(f\) be analytic on a region \(G\) and \(z_0 \in G\). Show that there exists a unique analytic function \(g\) on \(G\) such that, for all \(z \in G\setminus \{z_0\}\), \[g(z) = \dfrac {f(z) - f(z_0)}{z - z_0}.\]

Problem 11.98. Prove that if a function is continuous on a region \(G\) and analytic on \(G\setminus \{a\}\), then it is analytic throughout \(G\).

Problem 11.99. Compute the following integral using residues: \[\int ^{\infty }_{-\infty }\dfrac {dx}{(x^{2} + 4)(x^{2} + 3)} .\]

Problem 11.100. Find the central three terms \((c_{-1}, c_0, c_1)\) of the Laurent expansion of \[f(z) = \dfrac {1}{z(z-1)(3z-2)}\] valid in the annulus \(\tfrac {1}{6} < \left |z - \tfrac {1}{2}\right | < \tfrac {1}{2}\). Is the coefficient \(c_{-1}\) the residue of \(f(z)\) at one of its poles? Why?
