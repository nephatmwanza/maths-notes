A · Monogram tile

A serif “WJ” in a rounded tile beside a sans wordmark. The most conventional and the most robust — it survives being shrunk to a favicon, and the tile alone can stand in as an app icon later.

B · Integral J

The J is drawn as an integral sign — the same glyph reads as your father’s initial and as the symbol of the subject. Distinctive, and it means something. The risk is that it reads as “W∫” to a first-year who has not met integration yet.
